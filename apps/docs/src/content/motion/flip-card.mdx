A card with two faces that turns over in 3D, on hover, on click, or when you tell it to.

## Usage

```tsx
import { FlipCard, FlipCardBack, FlipCardFront } from '@/components/ui/flip-card'

<FlipCard trigger="click" aria-label="Pro plan" className="h-72 w-52">
  <FlipCardFront className="p-6">Pro, $12 a month</FlipCardFront>
  <FlipCardBack className="p-6">Everything that is in it</FlipCardBack>
</FlipCard>
```

The card takes the size of the larger face. The face out of view is hidden from screen readers and cannot take focus.

With `hover` or `click` the card is a toggle button with `aria-pressed`, so it turns over with Enter, Space or a tap as well. Keep links and buttons off its faces then. For faces with their own controls, use `manual` and drive `flipped` from a button next to it.

## Props

| Prop | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `trigger` | `'hover'` | `hover`, `click` or `manual`. |
| `direction` | `'horizontal'` | Turn around the vertical axis, or tip over with `vertical`. |
| `flipped`, `onFlippedChange` | | Controlled state. |
| `defaultFlipped` | `false` | Start on the back. |
| `duration` | `700` | Milliseconds for the turn. |
| `perspective` | `1200` | Perspective in pixels. Lower looks more dramatic. |

With `prefers-reduced-motion` the faces cross-fade instead of turning.
